# Real-device playtest checklist

These checks need a real iPhone and a person. Wear headphones and keep your eyes closed (or the phone face-down). The simulator and the automated tests cannot judge haptic feel, front/back clarity, comfort, or whether a person can actually navigate.

## Setup (sighted helper, once)

- [ ] Use wired headphones or AirPods. Note the model: ______
- [ ] Control Center → long-press the volume slider → Spatial Audio: **Off** (or "Fixed") for this app, so iOS does not re-spatialise the game's binaural sound.
- [ ] Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics: on.
- [ ] Volume at a comfortable level, with the helper's first speech line audible.
- [ ] Fresh install, or pause menu → Restart, so the practice runs.
- [ ] Optional: a second run with VoiceOver on (game gestures should still work).

## Practice (starting room)

- [ ] The intro is understandable and short enough.
- [ ] Holding one finger walks within a moment. Lifting stops immediately.
- [ ] Swipes turn reliably and the spoken direction matches what you expected. No walking happens from a swipe.
- [ ] Holding to walk goes straight even if your finger drifts.
- [ ] Footsteps sound wet and match your pace.
- [ ] You can find the dripping by turning, and tell when it is ahead.
- [ ] The cane tap: can you tell a close wall from a far one, and hear when there's no wall (an opening)?
- [ ] **Front/back:** with the drip behind you, do you know it's behind? (yes / no / unsure)
- [ ] Walking toward the drip makes it clearly louder and closer.
- [ ] Walking into a wall gives one firm knock (sound + vibration), not a buzz. Holding against the wall adds nothing more.
- [ ] Sliding along a wall at an angle gives a light tap and hand-scrape sounds.

## Exploring

- [ ] Two-finger tap gives a short, useful description that doesn't give away the route.
- [ ] Room names announced on entry help you know where you are. Swipe down turns you around.
- [ ] The small tick when facing along a wall is noticeable but not annoying.
- [ ] You can find the opening out of the first room by sound (other sounds come through it).
- [ ] The corridor feels different (wooden boards, narrower sound).
- [ ] **Wall sense:** walking along the corridor, you notice the side opening (the hush stops on one side, the fan hums in). Try with wall sense on and off.
- [ ] The vent room is recognisable by its hum and its metal floor. You can leave it and carry on.
- [ ] At the junction, the two routes sound different: a cold draft and outside sounds one way, a buzzing light the other.
- [ ] The storeroom is a safe dead end. You can turn around and find the way back.
- [ ] Retrace: walk back to the dripping room. Is it recognisable? Does the aid say "You've been here before"?
- [ ] Hidden sounds never seem to come from straight through a wall.
- [ ] The stairs feel like stairs (slower, stone steps). The air and outdoor sound grow.
- [ ] Reaching the top is unmistakable (door creak, rush of air, chord, vibration swell, then speech).
- [ ] Double-tap after escaping restarts cleanly.

## Pause, interruptions, comfort

- [ ] Two-finger hold pauses. Menu options are reachable eyes-free. Resume works.
- [ ] Toggle vibration off: the game is still playable, and the wall-alignment tick becomes a faint click.
- [ ] Press the Home/side button mid-walk, then return: still paused, no movement, a spoken reminder.
- [ ] Unplug or disconnect the headphones mid-walk: the game pauses and says so.
- [ ] Receive a call or trigger Siri: the game pauses. Afterwards, nothing has moved.
- [ ] Ten minutes of play: any hand fatigue, ear fatigue, or annoying repeated sounds?
- [ ] Any accidental walking or turning? When?
